Architecture Overview

What To Expect
GAMEPLAY · OFFLINE

No Account Sign-In

Deathventory: Ember Road is designed as a single-player experience with no in-game account registration, social/messaging features, or third-party advertising SDKs.

STORAGE · ON-DEVICE

Local Profile & Run Saves

Settings, camp progression, and suspended run saves are stored locally on your device inside the app’s local container rather than transmitted to a developer gameplay server.
